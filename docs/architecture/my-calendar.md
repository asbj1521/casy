# My calendar and Planlæg

_Moved out of CLAUDE.md (#155) word for word. Read it before changing this area, and keep it true as the code changes._

My calendar on both layouts, and planning from it on a phone (the paper pile). On a phone it is the home page (#169).

- The phone's home page (#169): on a phone and in the app there is one page, My calendar ("Hjem" / "Home", at `/`; `/calendar-overview` and `/plan` lead there, their `?plan` and `?calendars` kept), and no tab bar. Its months are the year searched; in the app they go on for three years (#231: `APP_CALENDAR_MONTHS`, `MonthView`'s `maxMonths`), added a year at a time as the end comes near. Past the year there is only what the phone holds, read once someone scrolls there (`phoneEventsFurtherQuery`, never sent), and the holidays: Google, Outlook and ICS are synced a year ahead, and nothing past it is searched or planned. Along its bottom, in glass over the months, float three buttons (`calendarView/HomeBar`): a round Aftaler button on the left (`CalendarCheck`, the count of votes waiting for your answer, `useEventsBadge`), a wide Planlæg pill in the middle, and a round Profil button on the right (`User`, the count of group invitations). The months scroll their last weeks clear of them and of the capsule above them (`--home-bar-clear` in index.css, MonthView's floating scroller); the row hides while a day, a sheet or the plan is open, but is never unmounted (opacity and `inert`), and sits under every sheet (z-20) except during Planlæg's send flight, when it comes back over the pile (z-65; `lib/homeBar.ts`, set by the pile and only reset once the pile is gone). Aftaler and Profil are sheets over the home page, in the address like `?plan` and `?calendars` (`?events`, `?profile`), so the phone's back closes them and `/events`, `/events/:id` and `/profile` on a phone lead there (the computer keeps its pages): `myEvents/phone/EventsSheet` (#177: the rows in three groups, `phoneEventGroups` in `lib/myEvents.ts`, tested: Svar nu, the votes waiting for your answer and one you suggested whose every date has a no; Kommende, soonest first; Tidligere, greyed, a week; each row a `DateTile`, the day as Apple Calendar's icon draws it, a vote's a stack; a vote to answer goes straight to `?vote=`, any other row pushes its screen inside the sheet as Profil's do, `?events=<id>`, `ui/push.ts` shared with ProfileSheet, `/events/:id` on a phone leading there: `DatedScreen`, an agreed event, its date tile and time, `DaySlice`, your day around it as the day view draws it with the event solid orange, its hours squeezed so the whole event fits with an hour either side (`DayColumns`' `fitHours`, five hours at the least), opening that day's day view, `PeopleRow`, everyone's face with a badge for their answer and the suggester starred, the place and note, then the actions as rows, Tilføj til min kalender pinned along the bottom, `AddToCalendar`'s `flow`; `VoteScreen`, a vote you have answered, a row per date with a bar filling with the yeses (paler for "rather not", nothing for no), the most yeses at the top so the leader is first (the rows gliding as answers change), ticked once half the group has answered, your answer at its end; under the dates the time left until it is settled ticking down (`Countdown`, `formatCountdown`) with Casys vurdering on the right (`AiReadout`'s `beside`), the place and note last, and Aflys or Forlad pinned along the bottom (a tap on the cross or tick is the change, so no other main action); leaving the sheet for the day view or a vote keeps where it was opened from) and `profile/ProfileSheet` (`ProfileHubList`; Groups is still its row). The profile's own screens (Indstillinger, Grupper and a group, Log ind og sikkerhed, Admin, Dine data, Slet konto) slide in inside the sheet (#174), as iOS pushes a screen: `BottomSheet`'s `stacked` (the header stays, each screen scrolls by itself), `back` ("‹ Profil", the title centred), the sheet one height throughout (85dvh), so a screen only slides; in the address as `?profile=<key>` (`lib/profileScreens.ts`; a group `?profile=groups/<id>`), each step a history entry (`sheetDepth` in its state, so Færdig steps back past all of them), and the old addresses (`/groups`, `/groups/:id`, `/profile/settings`, `/profile/password`, `/profile/account`, `/profile/admin`, `/profile/data`) lead there on a phone, carrying their query (`?password=new`). A link inside the sheet to one of them opens it in the sheet (`onClickCapture`), so the shared rows keep their `to`. The screens' contents are the pages' own (`PhoneGroupsContent`, `PhoneGroupContent`, `MyDataContent`, `PasswordContent`, ...). The help pages (Support, Sådan virker det, Vilkår, Privatliv) stay full pages. Every button opens its sheet from where it stands (`BottomSheet`'s `origin` and `edge`): Aftaler, Planlæg and Profil grow up out of their button at the bottom; Kalendere and the plus (Tell Casy) drop out of theirs at the top, the sheet only as tall as it needs (`edge="top"`). The sheet starts as the button (moved onto it and scaled, its corners elliptical so that once scaled they are the button's circle or capsule), grows into place with its content fading in over the second half, and shrinks back into the button as it closes, fading out over the last of it so the button shows through rather than the glass lying over its icon, held on the page by `usePresence` until it has; the browser's own animation engine runs it (`GrowingPanel`), measured before the first frame is drawn and held at the button until that frame has been, then played. The growing is cancelled on the effect's cleanup: in development React runs effects twice (StrictMode), and the second run measured a sheet already shrunk to the button and barely grew it (it seemed simply to appear in `ios:live`). An empty `origin` at either end slides instead: Planlæg's sheet slides away for the pile and back for "Ret planen". A sheet already open as the page appears (back from one of its screens, or a link) is shown in place (`arrived`), and still shrinks back as it closes. The sheets are put on the page ahead of their first press (Aftaler, Profil and Tell Casy 1.5 s after the home page opens or at a touch, Planlæg's heavy plan at a touch of the pill): a `lazy()` component suspends on its first render even with its code fetched, and React then holds back what a Suspense boundary reveals by up to 300 ms, which left the press with nothing to grow. The plan stays on the page until its sheet has shrunk into the pill (`onClosed`, Modal's `onExitComplete`), a new one each time it opens. Signed out, the website's phone header has a "Log ind" capsule (not on the landing page, which has its own); a signed-in phone's invite page has a way home.
