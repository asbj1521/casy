# My calendar and Planlæg

_Moved out of CLAUDE.md (#155) word for word. Read it before changing this area, and keep it true as the code changes._

My calendar on both layouts, and planning from it on a phone (the paper pile).

- My calendar (#104): `CalendarOverview` picks `PhoneCalendar` (below `md`) or `ComputerCalendar`, both fed by `useMyCalendarView` (your calendar over `SEARCH_WINDOW`, the holiday tick applied, the calendar list). The computer's is a Mac's month view filling the window beside the calendars sidebar, one month at a time over the 12 synced (‹ I dag ›, arrow keys, a trackpad's sideways swipe, non-passive like the swipe screen's); timed blocks are a dot, name and start, whole days a tinted bar (`isWholeDays`), as many lanes as the row height fits, weeks memoised (`WeekRow`, so `pickDay` and the week arrays stay stable); a clicked day opens a popover beside it, placed in percentages of the grid (a click elsewhere or Esc closes it). On a phone: It is the swipe screen's `MonthView` (Apple's month view, `pencil` and `marked` optional) filling the screen, scrolling inside itself, with the toolbar over it in a floating header (#136: `MonthView`'s `header` prop; the swipe screen doesn't pass one and keeps its headings): a translucent blurred block (`.glass-bar`, `--bar-alpha` in theme.css is how much shows through: 0.35 dark, 0.16 light, whose capsules are nearly clear and drawn by their edges, `--pill-*`) holding the toolbar in glass capsules (`.glass-pill`: "Kalendere" on the left, the plus and "I dag" in one capsule on the right; "I dag" glides the months back to this month, and in the app a tap at the very top of the screen glides them to the top (#168: `MonthViewHandle`, `lib/glideScroll.ts`, 0.35 s easing out, stopped by a touch, a jump with reduced motion; the list is never drawn afresh); WebKit's backdrop filter can't bend what is behind, so the glass blurs, brightens and rims it but never refracts; the Planlæg button above the tab bar is the same capsule), the name of the month on screen, which follows the scroll (`track`, state only on a change of month), and the weekdays, with the months scrolling under it; each month starts with its short name over its first day's column (`MONTH_LABEL_PX`) instead of a heading. The scroller leaves room for the header (`scroll-padding-top`, measured), so every scroll to a month goes through `monthScrollTop` (`lib/monthScroll.ts`, also the paper pile's), never a bare `offsetTop`; the pile's `MonthLayer` draws the same short name so its layers line up. In the app the months also pass under the clock: `useGlassTop` switches off the solid strip over the status bar (`html.glass-top`) and the page's box reaches up into the safe area; a tapped day (`selected`, orange; today is a grey circle, `todayTone`) lists its busy time in `calendarView/DayPanel` along the bottom, with no backdrop so other days stay tappable (the same day again, a blank part of the month or Færdig closes it); it is always mounted and only slides by a CSS transition, and `pickDay` must stay stable (`useCallback`) or every memoised month redraws on each tap, and the calendar list with its ticks, categories and priorities (`CalendarListPanel`) lives in the "Kalendere" sheet, which is in the address (`?calendars`) so the way back from Connected calendars returns to it, its intro sentence behind the (i). `MonthView`'s months are memoised (a tap redraws one or two, not twelve; and a month compares its items by what they draw in it, `sameItemsInMonth`, so a trip's bar changing length every 1.8 s pulse redraws only its own month, #162) and the formatted names and aria-labels come from cached `Intl` formatters (`dayLabel`, `monthName`, `monthShortName` in `lib/format.ts`; `toLocaleDateString` with options builds a new one per call, which was most of the JavaScript in a profile of the app); a trip Casy asks about glows through a ring of its own fading in and out (`animate-trip-pulse`, opacity only), never a `box-shadow` animation, which repainted the month every frame; and the weekday row sits above the months' scroller, not sticky inside it (iOS left a sliver over a sticky row). `BottomSheet` slides up as one `transform` string, which Framer Motion hands to the compositor; `y` was stepped from JavaScript and jumped on an iPhone. Ticks go through `useCalendarVisibility` on both layouts; `useMyCalendarDays` also hands over the calendars, error and truncation. In the iPhone app, this phone's own calendars are drawn from the phone, not the server (#110): the plugin's `readDetails` returns each event with its title, place and notes, `lib/phoneEvents.ts` puts one block per event (with `details`) in place of the server's merged blocks for the calendars whose `externalId` (the EventKit id, sent by `calendar-busy` for phone calendars only) belongs to this phone's connection, and `DayRow`, the month views and the swipe screen's days name them by title. Read again when the phone's calendars change; never persisted, never sent: the push is built from `read`, which has no details (a test guards it). Planned next: a Month | Day | List switch.
- Planning from My calendar (#126, phone only): a "Planlæg" button above the tab bar (hidden while a day or the plan is open) opens `?plan`, which mounts `components/planFromCalendar/PlanFromCalendar` (lazy, so My calendar alone never fetches a group's calendars), on `usePlanning`, the same hook as the scheduler, so it is one plan wherever begun. The design language is Apple's Liquid Glass as far as a web view allows (`.glass` in index.css, `--glass-*` in theme.css, light and dark; WKWebView can't refract, so it is frosted fill, bright edge, sheen). The sheet (`BottomSheet` `glass`, its `footer` pinning the way on; `PlanSetup`) is the settings by hand first (group, `StepSettings`, `DetailsStep`), with one shimmering "Planlæg med AI" pill for those AI is on for: pressed, the AI takes over the sheet (`AiConversation` with no "Tilføj detaljer"; "Indstil selv" backs out), and once every question is answered the settings come back filled in, guesses marked, with only "Beskriv på ny" (the AI starts over, it never refines). "Find dato" runs `PileScreen` over the calendar itself: My calendar scrolls to the first listed date's month (found by `MonthView`'s `data-month`), and each friend's calendar is tossed onto it as the same month view (`MonthLayer`, inert) on glass, from above and the side, left then right (`throwSide`): big and high at first, falling faster into the hit, its shadow on the table closing in (`frame.height`), quicker each time (`pileTimeline`, `PILE_TIMING`; slow on purpose, a deliberate wait the user wants for trust; don't shorten it), hitting with a squash, a small skid and a hop (`ThrownLayer`'s keyframes, memoised so a re-render never restarts a throw) that knocks the calendars under it (`jostle`, on its own layer): their busy time as bars in their colour with no words (never a calendar name or title), their name in a corner, each layer see-through enough that together they reach `PILE_OPACITY` (`layerAlpha`). The press pushes the rows down once from the middle out (`wrapRow`), no ripple, while the layers settle into line without a bounce and stay as they are, the group's calendar together (for now), on which the days free for everyone light up (`shine`, from `buildPile`'s `through`: the chart's own count; optional members get no layer). Then the aligned layers are swapped for one `SharedCalendar` (everyone's bars over every month up to the last listed date, lighter than a layer per person per month), and `DateList` lifts the dates out: it scrolls that calendar (a motion value, never by hand) until the next date's green circle (`data-shine`) is level with its empty row, stops, and the circle turns into the row (a FLIP, measured live; a date already level, such as two in one week, turns at once; one with no circle just appears), in date order. The pile names its month once, in My calendar's own big month name (#162): every layer and the shared calendar reach up to take it in (`pile/PileTitle`, measured from MonthView's `data-month-title`, `titleBand`), the small name over the 1st left empty so the weeks still line up, and while the shared calendar scrolls its band names the month at the top of the view, opaque so the page's own name never shows through. The header's buttons blur as the scroll starts (the close button stays above the blur), with "Gennemgår kalenderne" on the right of the month name; the blur over the calendar itself fades in only once all dates have arrived. Rows and the send flight's copies blur less while they move (`glass-moving`), which a phone redraws far more cheaply. Each date is then checked in turn under one line that stays put ("Gennemgår kalenderne"), a spinner on the row turning into a tick (offered: `rankCandidates`' chosen) or a cross, quicker and quicker (`checkTimes`), each date sliding to its place the moment it is checked (`liveOrder`: good ones rise among the best, others sink to the bottom; the number slot and the arrow are there from the start, so nothing shifts at the end), ending best first, numbered (`rankOrder`: the offered dates in the order `pickCandidates` chose them, `byPick`, then the rest by fewest giving something up, fewest edge notes, soonest; #119 adds the labels). A date opens to Casy's real checks: who can come, the evening before and the morning after (`edgeWarnings`), who would give something up, why it was or wasn't picked. Never invented findings: an "exam" is only for #119, from labels with consent (#120); no score until then. "Foreslå top N datoer" sends them with the suggester as `accepted` on each (changeable on My events). As the dates are sent (#102; the flight starts with the request rather than after the server's answer, which arrives under it, #162: `setBeforeLeaving`'s undo brings the list back if the send fails), the dates not offered swipe off to the right from the bottom row up, then the offered ones fly down into the Events tab, bottom one first, the tab (`data-tab-icon` in `TabBar`) bumping with a haptic on each arrival; then, once the server has answered too, My events opens. The list scrolls and would clip anything leaving it, so each row is cloned onto a layer of its own above the page (`lib/sendFlightPlay.ts`, the browser's animation engine on transform and opacity only) while the real rows are hidden; the timing is `lib/sendFlight.ts` (`flightPlan`, `FLIGHT_TIMING`, tested, about 1.7 s for eight dates). `usePlanning.setBeforeLeaving` is how the screen holds the navigation back until they have landed; without one, or with reduced motion, My events opens at once. No narration of the pile and no skip, by the user's choice; reduced motion goes straight to the checked list (every browser test runs so; `plan-from-calendar.spec.ts` turns motion on once). Haptics carry it (`lib/haptics.ts`, the app's own, the Vibration API elsewhere): a `thud` (heavy) as each calendar lands and at the press, a light `tick` every `SCROLL_TICK_PX` the calendar scrolls, an `answer` bump as each date drops into its row, a tick per check, `success` at the end. The checks say "Alle N med kalender kan" when some members check by hand, never more than Casy knows; with no date, a holiday offers the shorter stays that fit (`LaterDates`, which restarts the pile). `Modal` draws into `<body>` (a portal): inside a sheet that slid up with a transform, `fixed` was measured from the sheet, so a picker opened from it landed inside it.
